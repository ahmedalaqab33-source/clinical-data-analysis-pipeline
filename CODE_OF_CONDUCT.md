# Community Standards

This repository supports professional, respectful, and scientifically responsible collaboration.

## Expected conduct

Contributors and participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and constructively;
- critique methods, code, and evidence rather than individuals;
- disclose relevant limitations, assumptions, and conflicts when appropriate;
- protect participant privacy and avoid sharing restricted, identifiable, or sensitive health data;
- distinguish exploratory research from validated clinical decision support;
- document methodological changes that could affect interpretation or reproducibility.

## Unacceptable conduct

The following are not acceptable:

- harassment, discriminatory language, or personal attacks;
- deliberate misrepresentation of evidence or analytical results;
- submission of confidential, participant-level, credential, or institutionally restricted material;
- attempts to bypass data-governance, security, or reproducibility safeguards.

## Scientific integrity

Changes that affect variable definitions, inclusion criteria, statistical methods, model specification, estimands, or interpretation should be clearly justified and documented. Reproducibility and transparency take priority over cosmetic complexity.

## Reporting concerns

For sensitive concerns, security issues, or conduct matters, contact the repository maintainer privately at ahmedalaqab33@gmail.com rather than posting confidential details publicly.
